How insurers limit what your homeowner’s policy covers
Over the past decade, Florida insurers with the approval of state regulators have put stricter limits on how much a homeowner can get paid for a damage claim. The language imposing these limits varies by company. Here are some key examples.
WATER DAMAGE CAP
A $10,000 maximum payout on water damage, once rare, is now common. Some companies impose it only on older homes.
American Integrity Insurance Co., 2026

OTHER LIMITATIONS

Sl0,000 limit on nonweather water losses - If your policy includes the Limited Water Damage endorsement, your policy contains a limit on covered losses caused by accidental discharge or water of $10,000. Payment for reasonable emergency measures will be deducted from the $10,000 limit on coverage. Please read your policy carefully for specific information.

Citizens Property Insurance, 2025

1. A $10,000 limit on coverage applies and is the most we will pay for: a. Each covered direct physical loss from all water or steam in paragraphs 2.c.(6) and 2.c.(9) above; and b. All cosmetic and aesthetic damage, which occurs in the same loss as 3.a. above, including any repair or replacement of items to match quality, color, or size. Payment for Reasonable Emergency Measures under SECTION I Property Coverages Paragraph E.1., which occurs in the same loss as 3.a. above, will be deducted from the $10,000 limit on coverage.

NOTE: Citizens removes the water cap for policyholders who agree to participate in its managed-repair network, which allows the insurer to choose the contractor to fix any damage.

EXAMPLE 2: Either insurer or homeowner can request appraisal
Manatee Insurance Exchange, 2023

B. Appraisal.

Appraisal is an alternate dispute resolution method to address and resolve disagreement regarding the amount of the covered loss.

1. If you and we do not agree on the amount of loss, then either may request an appraisal of the loss. In this event, each party will choose a competent and impartial appraiser within 20 days after receiving a written request from the other. The appraisers will separately set the amount of the loss. If the appraisers submit a written report of an agreement to us, the amount agreed upon will be the amount of the loss. If the two appraisers fail to agree, they will choose a competent and impartial umpire, and failing to agree upon such umpire within 15 days, you or we may request that the choice be made by a judge of a court of record located in the county described in the location of the "residence premises" of your Declarations. The two appraisers will submit their differences to the umpire. An award signed by any two will set the amount of loss.

MATCHING LIMITS
Many companies have adopted tight limits on what can be called "cosmetic" repairs — but homeowners often find such repairs critical to restoring roofs, floors or cabinetry to pre-damage conditions.
Slide Insurance Co., 2024

For HOMEOWNERS 3 -SPECIAL FORM:

The total limit of liability for Coverages A and B is 1% of the Coverage A limit of liability for repairs or replacements of any undamaged part of the building or its components solely to match repairs made to damage as a result of a covered loss.

This limitation does not increase the Coverage A or Coverage B limits of liability shown on the Declarations page, nor does it apply to damage otherwise limited or excluded.
